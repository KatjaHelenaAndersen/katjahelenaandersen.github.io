Growing up, I was what is often called a “third culture kid.” Born in Sri Lanka to a Sri Lankan mother and a Danish father, I spent my childhood between Sri Lanka, Germany and Singapore before settling in Denmark at 17, where I completed the International Baccalaureate in Aalborg.

I began my university studies in mathematics at Aarhus University, but soon found myself drawn to economics, particularly applied economics and its ability to answer real-world questions with data. After a gap year at Uldum Højskole, I began my studies in economics at SDU in 2019, and I have happily stayed ever since, now as a PhD student at the start of an academic path.

Outside of research, I have more interests than I have time for. I love sports of all kinds, aerial fitness above all, and I’m always eager to try something new, whether that’s a new hobby, a new album or a new book.

Feel free to reach out if you’d like to talk about anything!
